How AGI Talent works

Two focused flows keep the experience simple: one for finding openings and one for collecting hiring manager intake.

AI Openings

Browse a curated list of research and infrastructure roles. This is the public-facing discovery path for candidates and partners who want to see active opportunities quickly.

  • Current openings surfaced from the source feed
  • Built for quick scanning on desktop and mobile
  • Designed to turn interest into qualified leads

Hiring Manager

Use the intake flow to capture role context, team priorities, and hiring needs. This keeps the search aligned before outreach starts.

  • Structured intake for research and infra teams
  • Centralized details for faster coordination
  • Reduces back-and-forth during sourcing
